## Project 1: Retail Sales Analysis Dashboard

### Overview

An interactive Excel dashboard built to analyze retail sales performance across regions, product categories, customer segments, and monthly trends.

### Key Metrics

- Total Sales: $5,356,476.90
- Total Profit: $1,692,752.65
- Profit Margin: 31.60%
- Total Orders: 3,000
- Units Sold: 16,855

### Analysis & Visualizations

- Monthly Sales Trend
- Sales by Region
- Sales by Customer Segment
- Sales by Category
- Profit by Category
- KPI Dashboard
- Business Insights

### Key Insights

- South region generated the highest sales at $1.47M.
- Technology generated the highest sales at $3.03M.
- Technology generated the highest profit at approximately $963K.
- Overall profit margin was 31.60%.
